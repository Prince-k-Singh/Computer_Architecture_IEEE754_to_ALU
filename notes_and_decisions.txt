Suppose:

fraction = 101100...

The stored fraction is:

101100...

but for a normal IEEE-754 number, the actual significand is:

1.101100...

That means internally we'll eventually convert:

52-bit fraction

into:

53-bit significand

for FP64:

1 + 52 fraction bits
────────────────────
        53 bits
