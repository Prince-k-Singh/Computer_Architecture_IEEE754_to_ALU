VVVIP

FP64 ADD/SUB currently truncate the extra significand precision
instead of implementing full IEEE-754 round-to-nearest-even.
Randomized verification shows 98.67% agreement with the
binary64 reference model; all observed ADD/SUB mismatches are
1-ULP rounding differences. MUL and DIV show 100% agreement
for the current 10,000-test random-normal dataset.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------




Suppose:

fraction = 101100...

The stored fraction is:

101100...

but for a normal IEEE-754 number, the actual significand is:

1.101100...

That means internally we'll eventually convert:

52-bit fraction

into:

53-bit significand

for FP64:

1 + 52 fraction bits
────────────────────
        53 bits
